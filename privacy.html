Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

1. Who we are

ClipNada is a private desktop application operated by Nadamedia (Poland). It is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own social media accounts. Contact: sailor1720@gmail.com.

2. What data the app accesses

When the owner connects his accounts, ClipNada accesses only the data needed for its features:

  • YouTube (Google): channel name and ID, uploading and deleting the owner's videos, video and channel statistics (YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API).
  • Gmail (Google, read-only): searching the owner's inbox only for emails from YouTube about copyright claims, strikes or policy notices, in order to show an alert. Email content is not stored beyond the subject, sender and a short snippet of those notices.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta): list of the owner's Pages and linked Instagram accounts, publishing Reels and reading their statistics.
  • TikTok: basic profile information, publishing the owner's videos and reading their statistics.

3. How the data is used and stored

All data and access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The data is used only to publish the owner's content and to display statistics and alerts to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

ClipNada's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Third-party services

Text of video transcripts may be sent to Google Gemini (Google Cloud) to generate titles, descriptions and translations, and audio may be sent to ElevenLabs for voice-over generation. No account data from YouTube, Gmail, Meta or TikTok is sent to these services.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the owner uses the app. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Meta: Facebook Settings → Business integrations
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

Deleting the app's local data folder permanently removes all stored data and tokens. To request deletion of any data, contact sailor1720@gmail.com.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
