ClipNada

A private desktop tool by Nadamedia

ClipNada is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used only by its owner to manage his own gaming channels and pages on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok (Polish and English versions).

What it does:

  • prepares short vertical clips from gameplay content the owner has permission to use,
  • adds subtitles, titles, descriptions and hashtags,
  • publishes the clips to the owner's own accounts,
  • shows view and follower statistics of the owner's own accounts in a private dashboard,
  • alerts the owner about platform notices (e.g. copyright or policy emails from YouTube).

ClipNada is not offered to the public and has no other users.
